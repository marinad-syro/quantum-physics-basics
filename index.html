Core concepts

Four ideas that changed how we understand reality

◌

Wave-particle duality

Electrons and other tiny objects can behave like waves or particles, depending on how we measure them.

✦

Superposition

Before measurement, a quantum system can exist as a combination of multiple possible states.

△

Uncertainty

Some properties, such as position and momentum, cannot both be known with perfect precision.

∿

Entanglement

Quantum objects can become linked so that their measurement results remain correlated across distance.
